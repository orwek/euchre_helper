# euchre_helper
A PWA to make playing Euchre easier.

While playing Euchre at a recent family reunion, my father and I decided it would be a good idea to make an app to make it easier to score, show what suit is trump, keep track of who dealt, who took the bid, as well as the rules and some other information to make teaching new players easier too. The end result is what you see before you.

This PWA includes:
- Scoring tabs for 2, 3, or 4 player games
- Modified rules for 2 & 3 player
- Tiles you can mark/unmark by tapping for suit, bid, and dealer
- Full game rules that I originally learned, based on the old PalmOS game.
